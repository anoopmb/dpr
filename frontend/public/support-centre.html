End-User Guide

· Welcome

This guide explains every screen in the system, what each field is for, who can do what, and how data flows from a worker's attendance mark on a job site all the way through to a billed running bill. It is written directly against how the system actually behaves today — if a feature described elsewhere (a workflow step, a button) doesn't exist yet, this guide says so rather than guessing.

This is a Daily Progress Report (DPR), Measurement Book (MB), and Billing system for construction project execution and commercial tracking. In short, it lets field staff record what happened on site each day — work executed, materials and machinery consumed, worker attendance, and measurements against the Bill of Quantities (BOQ) — and lets project management/QS/finance staff review, approve, and turn that into running bills, while admins keep the reference data (materials, rates, users) and the organization's settings up to date.

How to use this guide: use the sidebar to jump to any screen. Each screen's section states its route, which roles can open it, its purpose, every field/button you'll see, and what happens when you submit. Workflow sections include diagrams showing exactly how a record moves between statuses.

1 Getting Started

Signing in

You'll be given a Login ID and password by your administrator. There is no self-service sign-up — every account is created by an Admin on the Users screen (see Users & Roles).

The sidebar

The left sidebar groups every screen you have access to under five headings — Project, Resources, Finance, Reports, and Administration — plus a Dashboard link on its own. You only see the groups/items your role is allowed to open; the rest are simply not shown (see Roles & Permissions for the full breakdown). On a phone, the sidebar becomes a slide-out drawer opened with the menu icon at the top left.

Light / Dark theme

Click your avatar (top right) → choose Light, Dark, or System under the Theme section. "System" follows your device's own light/dark setting automatically.

Notifications

The bell icon (top right) shows your unread count and opens your notification list — a dropdown on desktop, a full-screen panel on mobile. See Notifications & Approvals for exactly what triggers a notification and who receives it.

Installing as an app

A banner may appear inviting you to install this as an app on your device (a Progressive Web App) so it behaves like a native app with its own icon, no browser address bar, and works offline for already-loaded pages. On Android/desktop Chrome, the banner has an Install button that does this directly. On iPhone/iPad (Safari has no install button), it instead shows the manual steps: tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen." Dismissing the banner only hides it for that browsing session.

Allowing push notifications

Shortly after you sign in, your browser may ask "Allow notifications?" — this lets a notification reach you even when the app isn't open in front of you, similar to a phone's native notifications. This prompt appears automatically; there's no separate in-app switch for it. Allow it if you want background alerts; declining is harmless — you'll still see everything in the in-app bell.

Your profile

Click your avatar → My Profile to view your details, change your name/photo, change your password, or review your recent activity. See Profile for details.

3 Roles & Permissions

There are five roles. Every screen and every approval action is gated by role — this table is the quick reference; exact gates are repeated in each screen's own section below.

RoleWhat they're generally for
Site Engineer The on-site field role. Logs Today's DPR, materials/machinery used, worker attendance, records measurements, and requests material indents. Has no access to BOQ setup, billing, or admin screens.
Project Manager Sets up and manages projects (BOQ, assignments, geofence/verification settings), gives the first stage of measurement approval, approves material indents, and views every report.
Quantity Surveyor Gives the final measurement approval and locks measurements (making them billable), and generates running bills.
Store Keeper Approves and issues material indents, manages stock locations, and logs expenses.
Administrator Everything — every screen above, plus Master Data, Users, Settings, and Audit Logs. Can also perform any other role's approval actions.
Note: the two "My ..." field screens — My Measurements and My Material Indents — have no role restriction at all in the system; any signed-in user can open them, though in practice there's nothing of "yours" to see there unless you've actually recorded a measurement or raised an indent yourself.

4 Field Operations

These are the mobile-oriented screens under the "Field" area, available to Site EngineerProject ManagerAdmin (the My Measurements / My Material Indents screens are open to any role).

Shared mechanics across every Field screen

  • Project selector — nothing works until you pick a project at the top; your choice is remembered across screens and even after you close the browser.
  • One shared entry date — Today's DPR is the only screen with a date picker. Whatever date you pick there is the date Materials Used, Machinery Used, and Measurement Entry all silently use too (shown as a read-only "Entry date: ..." line on those screens) — you never have to pick the date twice.
  • Attendance gate — Today's DPR, Materials Used, Machinery Used, and Measurement Entry are all locked behind one rule: you must mark at least one worker present today (the real calendar date — this check is not affected by back-dating your entry date) before any of their content appears. If you haven't yet, you'll see "Mark attendance first" with a button straight to the Attendance screen.

Today's DPR /field/dpr-todaySite EngineerPMAdmin

Purpose: the daily report for a project — one record per project per calendar date, covering work executed, a daily activity summary, and any issues. "DPR" = Daily Progress Report.

If no DPR exists yet for the selected date, you'll see a small form: Supervisor Name (pre-filled with your name, editable), and a "No work today (rain / holiday / shutdown)" toggle — switching it on reveals a reason dropdown (Rain / Holiday / Sunday / Other, with a free-text box for Other). Button: Start Today's DPR (reads "Start DPR for {date}" if you've picked a past date). The date picker next to the page title only allows dates from the project's start date up to today.

Once a DPR exists, the title shows a status tag — DRAFT or SUBMITTED. While DRAFT:

  • Supervisor Name and the no-work toggle/reason stay editable. If you try to flip a day to "no work" after already logging production/material/machinery entries, you'll get: "Cannot mark as a no-work day: this DPR already has production, material, or machinery entries."
  • If "no work" is on, the rest of the page collapses to one line stating the reason.
  • Otherwise you get three tabs:
    • Production — every BOQ item for the project, with a Today's Qty input and a Chainage text field (e.g. "2/130–2/390") per row; Amount is computed automatically as qty × rate. An optional "Today's Activity" text box above the table becomes the report's daily summary. Button: Save Production Entries (saves every row you've touched).
    • Other Tasks — just quick links to the other Field screens.
    • Issues — log a problem: Description (required), Severity (Low/Medium/High/ Critical), optional photo. Button: Log Issue. Issues are append-only — no edit/delete.

Bottom actions (hidden once submitted): Submit Today's DPR (irreversible — flips status to SUBMITTED; there is no further approval stage on the DPR header itself) and Delete this DPR (only while DRAFT — deletes every production/material/machinery/issue entry logged for that date too).

Validation: the report date must be on or after the project's start date and not in the future — the system checks this even though the date picker also blocks it.

Materials Used /field/materials

Purpose: record material consumption for the selected entry date — opening this screen creates that day's DPR header automatically if it doesn't exist yet.

Pick a Material (searchable) — its Unit and Rate auto-fill from the Material master record but both stay editable. Enter Quantity. Button: Add Entry. The rate you save is locked to that entry — if someone later changes the material's master rate, your already-logged entries keep the rate that was true on the day you logged them.

Each entry below shows as "{material} — {qty} {unit} @ ₹{rate}/{unit}" with Edit/Delete — available only while that day's DPR is still DRAFT.

Machinery Used /field/machinery

Same pattern as Materials Used: pick a Machine (auto-fills Hire Rate, editable), enter Hours Used (must be more than 0 and at most 24). Same locked-rate behaviour, same Edit/Delete- while-DRAFT rule.

Attendance /field/workers

Purpose: mark which workers are on site today. This is the one Field screen never blocked by the attendance gate (it's how you satisfy that gate in the first place).

A Your Location card auto-captures your GPS and — if the project has a site boundary configured — tells you whether you're inside or outside it (outside is flagged, not blocked).

+ Add New Worker expands a small form (Full Name, Category, Phone). Whether saving it marks them present immediately depends on the project's verification settings (see below).

Which check-in flow you see depends on the project's own Photo/GPS verification settings (configured per-project under Projects → Settings, not on the global org Settings page):

  • Verification required (photo and/or GPS on): pick a worker, capture a photo if required, tap Check In. Already-checked-in workers are greyed out. A second check-in attempt for the same worker/day is rejected: "This worker is already checked in for today."
  • Verification off: a simpler Bulk Mark Present card — multi-select any number of workers, tap Mark Present.

A "Today's attendance" list at the bottom shows everyone marked present, tagged In/Out of boundary (if verified) or Auto-marked (if bulk-marked).

Measurement Entry /field/measurements

Purpose: record a Measurement Book entry against a BOQ item by entering physical dimensions rather than typing a quantity directly.

Pick a BOQ Item — its Work Type determines which dimension fields appear (Length, Width, Depth, Breadth, Height, Thickness, or Diameter, in metres/mm as labelled) and how they combine into a quantity:

FormulaComputes quantity as
PRODUCTevery dimension field multiplied together
AREA_LHLength × Height specifically
LENGTH_ONLYjust the length (billed by running length)

A live Computed Quantity box updates as you type. Add an optional Chainage / Location and an optional evidence photo (which also captures GPS at the moment you submit). Button: Submit Measurement (becomes "Save & Add Another" after your first one). New measurements start as DRAFT.

Validation: quantity must be greater than 0, and your running total against that BOQ item (existing + this entry) can't exceed the tendered quantity by more than the organization's allowed tolerance percentage (set on the Settings screen) — you'll see "Could not save measurement — check it does not exceed the BOQ tolerance" if it does.

My Measurements /field/my-measurements

Every measurement you personally recorded on this project, searchable by item/description/ chainage. Only DRAFT rows get action buttons: Submit (enters the approval chain — see Measurement Book for the full workflow), Edit, and Delete. Anything past DRAFT is view-only here — approval itself happens on the desktop Measurements screen, not in the field view.

My Material Indents /field/my-indents

Purpose: request materials, rather than log what you've already consumed. New Indent: Source Type (Head Office Store or Site-to-Site Transfer), Source Location, and one or more material lines (Material, Unit, Requested Qty).

From here you can only Submit a draft, Cancel your own draft/submitted indent, and Receive against line items once it's been issued. Approving, rejecting, or issuing happen on the management side — see Material Indents below for the full lifecycle.

5 Project & Commercial Management

Projects /projectsPMAdmin

The master list of projects. New Project: Department, Project Name, Tender No, Agreement No (optional), Contract Value, Start Date, Completion Date, and two toggles — "Require photo for attendance" / "Require GPS for attendance" (both default on). The table shows Tender No, Name, Contract Value, dates, and Status (ACTIVE COMPLETED SUSPENDED), searchable by tender no/name/agreement no.

Row actions: Edit (name, agreement no, contract value, completion date, status — tender no/department/start date can't be changed after creation), Settings, Assign, and Delete (Admin only, and only possible if the project has no BOQ/DPR/measurement data yet — otherwise you'll get "Cannot delete — this project has related data").

Settings

Attendance: the Require-photo / Require-GPS switches — this is exactly what decides which check-in flow workers see on the Field Attendance screen.

Site Boundary (Geofence): an interactive map to set the site's centre point and radius (metres) — by click, drag, or "Use My Location". Check-ins outside the radius are flagged, never blocked.

Assign

Set the Primary Project Manager and Primary Site Engineer (single dropdowns), plus any number of Additional Assignments (role + user). This is also how Quantity Surveyors and Store Keepers get linked to a project at all — and it's exactly how the system decides who gets notified for approvals on that project (see Notifications).

BOQ & Templates /boqPMQSAdmin

Two tabs: BOQ and Templates.

BOQ tab: sections (each a colored banner row with an item count, with its own Add Item/Edit/Delete) containing items — Item No, Description, Work Type, Unit, Tendered Qty, Consumed Qty, Rate, Tendered Value — followed by a Section Total row. Toolbar: Add Section / Add BOQ Item (Section, Item No, Description, Work Type, Unit, Tendered Qty, Rate), and a second row — Download Template (blank .xlsx), Import (validates work type/unit lookups before committing, shows per-row errors), Export, Apply Template, Save as Template.

Templates tab: org-wide, reusable BOQ sets (not tied to one project). Saving snapshots the current project's full BOQ including its quantities and rates; applying a template to a different project imports all its items (skipping any item number that already exists, auto-creating new sections) — because the rate/quantity came from whichever project the template was made from, you should review and adjust them after applying to a different project.

Deleting: a section must have its items reassigned elsewhere first; an item can't be deleted once it has any recorded measurements against it ("Only items with no recorded measurements can be deleted").
Variation Orders (extra quantity allowance beyond the normal tolerance limit) exist as a concept in the underlying data model, but there is currently no screen anywhere to create or view one — so in practice, the tolerance percentage on the Settings screen is the only ceiling that applies; there's no in-app way to raise it for a specific item today.

Workers & Attendance /workersPMAdmin

Two tabs. Worker Master: add/edit workers (Full Name, Worker Category, Phone, Contractor — explicitly reference-only text, not linked to any billing) with an Active/Inactive toggle, searchable by name/phone/contractor. Attendance Log: a read-only history of check-ins — Date, Worker, Check-in Time, and a Within/Outside-boundary tag — searchable by worker name.

Expenses /expensesPMStore KeeperAdmin

Simple local/petty expense logging — there is no approval workflow here at all. Fields: Category, Amount, Date, Vendor Name (reference-only — no vendor master or payment tracking), Remarks. Every row can be edited or deleted regardless of age, searchable by category/vendor/remarks.

Running Bills /billingQSPMAdmin

Generate Running Bill: Bill Number, Bill Date, Period From/To, and a checkbox-selectable table of locked, not-yet-billed measurements only. Each selected measurement's value (qty × BOQ rate) is summed into the bill total, and every measurement you select is marked billed so it can't be pulled into a second bill.

Record Payment (hidden once a bill is fully paid): Amount Received, Received Date, Reference No — checked against the remaining balance.

In practice, a bill only ever has two states: DRAFT and PAID. The data model defines SUBMITTED and APPROVED states too, and you may see tag colours defined for them, but there is currently no button or workflow step anywhere that sets a bill to either of those — a bill is created directly as DRAFT and jumps straight to PAID once payments received equal the total amount.

When a bill becomes fully paid, the project's PMs and whoever generated the bill are notified.

6 Reporting & Analytics

Dashboard /dashboard

The home screen — its content changes entirely depending on your role and whether a project is selected.

No project selected: Admins see a Portfolio Overview — KPIs (Total/Active Projects, Portfolio Contract, Total Outstanding), a Project Status donut, a Top-10 "Contract vs Billed vs Received" chart (click a bar to drill into that project), and a searchable, sortable All Projects table with totals. Every other role just sees "Select a project."

Project selected:

  • Admin / PM — KPIs (Contract Value, Total Billed, Payment Received, Outstanding), plus a Daily P&L Trend chart, Cost Breakdown donut, Expenses by Category, Measurement Status donut, BOQ Progress by Section, Attendance Trend, Issues by Severity, Material Indents status donut, and a Bills Pipeline chart.
  • Quantity Surveyor — KPIs (Tendered Value, Consumed Value, BOQ Completion %, Unbilled Measurements), plus BOQ Progress by Section, Measurement Status, and Bills Pipeline.
  • Site Engineer — KPIs (DPR Entries, Work Days, No-Work Days, Open Issues), plus Issues by Severity, Attendance Trend, and a Production Progress summary card.
  • Store Keeper — KPIs (Total Indents, Pending Approval, Issued, Received), plus a Material Indent status donut and a Pending Approvals mini-table.

Most charts have their own date-range filter (7 days / 30 days / This month / Year-to-date / Project to date).

Analytics /analyticsPMQSAdmin

A portfolio-wide view across every project at once (no project selector). KPIs, a sortable "by contract / billed / outstanding" Top-10 chart (bars drill into that project's Dashboard), a Billing & Collection Efficiency chart (Billed % and Collected % per project), top-3 project cards, and a fully sortable, searchable All Projects table with a totals row.

Production Progress /production-progressPMQSAdmin

One project, one as-of date (defaults to today, can't be set in the future). Grouped by BOQ section: Item No, Description, Unit, Rate, Agreed Qty/Amt, Executed Qty/Amt, Remaining Qty, % Done — then a Section Total row, then a Grand Total at the very bottom.

What "Executed" means precisely: it's the running total of whatever has landed in DPR production entries up to that date — which happens either because someone typed it directly into Today's DPR's Production tab, or because a measurement was Locked (locking automatically feeds that quantity into the day's DPR production entry).

Profitability /profitabilityPMAdmin

One project, a date range (This Week / This Month / Project to Date, or pick your own). Four stat cards — Production Value, Total Cost, Net P&L, Margin % — a Cost Breakdown bar, and a Daily Breakdown table.

Exact formulas — read carefully, this report's cost basis is narrower than the Monthly DPR Report's:
  • Production Value = the same DPR production value used everywhere (qty × BOQ rate, summed per day)
  • Total Cost = Machinery + Materials + Expenses only — Labour/attendance cost is not included here
  • Net P&L = Production Value − Total Cost
  • Margin % = Net P&L ÷ Production Value × 100

DPR History /dpr-historyPMQSAdmin

Every day's DPR for a project, searchable by supervisor/activity text. Each row is classified as:

Day TypeWhen it applies
No Workthe day was explicitly marked no-work
Pre-work / Mobilizationno production work items logged, but at least one material or machinery entry exists
Production Dayeverything else

Columns: Date, Status, Day Type, Supervisor, Today's Activity, and an Issues count tag (red if any were logged).

Monthly DPR Report /dpr-monthly-reportPMQSAdmin

The most complete single report in the system — one project, one month — built as several sections:

  • A. Daily Production — every BOQ item, with Opening (as of month start), a Qty & Amount pair of columns for every single day in the month, and Closing (as of month end), frozen Item No/ Description columns so they stay visible while you scroll sideways through the days.
  • B. Expenditure — four sub-tables, each day-by-day with its own Total row:
    1. Material Consumption
    2. Machinery
    3. Labour — worker categories costed as attendance count × category daily rate
    4. Expenses — grouped by category, amount only (no quantity)
  • C. Summary — per day: Total Income (= production value), Total Expense (= materials + machinery + labour + expenses — this one does include labour, unlike Profitability above), Net Profit/Loss, Profit/Loss % (= net profit ÷ total expense × 100 — note this is a different denominator from Profitability's Margin %), and a running Cumulative Income/Expense since the project's actual start date, shown only on week-boundary rows (every 7th day, and the month's last day).

The Export button downloads a multi-section spreadsheet mirroring this exact layout, scoped strictly to whichever month you have selected.

Why this report's numbers can differ from Profitability's: they're deliberately separate calculations — this report includes labour cost and divides by total expense; the Profitability report excludes labour cost and divides by production value. Both are correct for what they're named; they're just not directly comparable figures.

Worker Earnings /worker-earningsPMQSAdmin

One project, a date range and an optional worker-category filter. One row per worker: Days Present (count of attendance records in range) × that worker's category daily rate = Total Earnings.

This is explicitly not a payroll system — the report itself states "informational costing only." It's an estimate for tracking labour cost, not a wage/payroll record.

Daily Labour Log /daily-labour-logPMQSAdmin

The same cost basis and the same "not a payroll system" disclaimer as Worker Earnings, but broken out day-by-day instead of summarized per worker — one row per (date, worker) pair.

Audit Logs /audit-logsAdmin only

A searchable trail of every Create/Update/Delete/Status-Change action recorded in the system. Filters: Entity Type (a live list of whatever entity types actually exist in the log — not a fixed list), Action, Actor (any user), and a date range. Each row shows When/Actor/Action/Entity Type/Entity ID, with a View button opening the full Before/After data side by side. Paging is Previous/Next only — there's no total record count shown.

9 Glossary

BOQ (Bill of Quantities)
The contracted list of work items for a project — each with a tendered quantity and rate. Everything else (measurements, billing, progress) is tracked against it.
MB (Measurement Book)
The traditional construction-industry term for the record of measured, verified work quantities — this system's digital equivalent is the Measurements screen.
DPR (Daily Progress Report)
The single daily record per project covering production, materials, machinery, and issues.
Tendered Qty / Consumed Qty
Tendered = the contracted quantity for a BOQ item. Consumed = the cumulative quantity locked against it so far via approved measurements.
Tolerance %
How far (as a percentage above the tendered quantity) a measurement is still allowed to go before being rejected — set on the Settings screen.
Chainage
A free-text location reference along a linear work stretch (e.g. road/drain), e.g. "2/130–2/390".
Geofence
The site boundary (centre point + radius) used to flag whether a worker's attendance check-in happened inside or outside the actual job site.
Snapshotted rate
When you log a material or machinery entry, the current master rate is copied onto that entry permanently — later changes to the master rate never retroactively change already-logged costs.

10 Troubleshooting & FAQ

"Mark attendance first" is blocking a Field screen

You haven't yet marked any worker present today on this project. Go to Attendance, mark at least one worker present, then the screen you wanted unlocks.

"This worker is already checked in for today"

Attendance allows one check-in per worker per calendar day — they're already marked, no further action needed.

"Could not save measurement — check it does not exceed the BOQ tolerance"

Your recorded quantity would push that BOQ item's total consumed quantity past the allowed tolerance percentage above its tendered quantity. Double-check the dimensions you entered, or speak to your Project Manager/Admin about the item's tendered quantity.

"Cannot delete — this project has related data"

A project can only be deleted while it has zero BOQ items, DPRs, or measurements recorded against it. This is a safety guard, not a bug — there is no way around it from the UI.

Why don't I see a screen/menu item that a colleague has?

Every screen is gated by role — see Roles & Permissions. If you believe you need access, ask your Administrator to either change your role or assign you to the relevant project.

I changed a project's attendance verification settings but nothing changed

Those toggles live on that specific project's Settings drawer (Projects → Settings), not the global Administration → Settings screen — double check you edited the right one.

Why do Profitability and the Monthly DPR Report show different profit numbers for the same period?

They use deliberately different formulas — see the note under Monthly DPR Report. Profitability excludes labour cost and measures margin against production value; the Monthly DPR Report includes labour cost and measures it against total expense.
